Qualitative drivers
What actually moves the thesis over the next 12–24 months
Tailwinds bull case
  • ▲Hyperscaler capex sprint. ~$690B of 2026 AI infrastructure spend — Amazon $220B, Google ~$200B — with cloud capacity still supply-constrained.
  • ▲Blackwell → Rubin cadence. Blackwell Ultra ramping; annual architecture cadence keeps the performance-per-watt lead and pricing power intact.
  • ▲Networking flywheel. NVLink/Spectrum + InfiniBand attach nearly doubled; the rack-scale system, not just the GPU, is the moat.
  • ▲CUDA software lock-in. Two decades of developer tooling raise switching costs against every merchant and custom-silicon rival.
  • ▲Fortress balance sheet. $72B net cash funds buybacks, supply pre-payments, and strategic stakes without dilution.
  • ▲China optionality. H20/H200 export licenses exist; any realized China revenue is upside not yet in numbers.
Headwinds bear case
  • ▼Custom ASIC encroachment. Broadcom AI-ASIC revenue $8.4B/qtr (+106%) with a $73B backlog; Google TPU, AWS Trainium, MS Maia pull hyperscaler share in-house.
  • ▼AMD MI400. HBM4-class memory advantage; AMD secured ~6GW commitments each from OpenAI and Meta for the 2026 launch.
  • ▼Customer concentration. A handful of hyperscalers drive ~50% of revenue — capex digestion by any one dents the model.
  • ▼Margin normalization. Gross margin already stepped down from ~75% to ~71%; mix and competition cap re-expansion.
  • ▼China zeroed out. Effectively $0 realized China revenue vs ~$5B/yr prior; local rivals (Huawei) entrenching.
  • ▼"AI bubble" / valuation. $5.2T cap prices sustained hyper-growth; any capex-cycle wobble compresses the multiple fast.

The two things to watch. Neither breaks the thesis in the base case, but both cap the multiple.
