1. Add IEF — 7-10yr US Treasuries  corr +0.16 to S&P block
Lowest correlation of every candidate tested, at just 4.4% annualized vol. The cleanest ballast against the 69% equity concentration.
2. Add TLT — 20+yr US Treasuries  corr +0.17 to S&P block
Longer duration gives more convex upside if the Fed eases into a growth scare. Caveat from the data: TLT is 0.91 correlated with IEF, so size the two together as one duration sleeve rather than stacking both at full weight. Rate risk is live — the 10Y sits near a 1-year high at 4.72%.
3. Scale up IGLN gold toward ~12-15%  corr +0.33 to S&P block
Gold correlates only +0.42 to Treasuries, so it is a genuinely independent hedge — and it covers the inflation / debasement / Middle-East (Strait of Hormuz) scenario that bonds do not. It is already the book's best diversifier at 8.4%.
What the numbers rule out: ex-US equity ETFs are not hedges for this book — VEA correlates +0.65 and VWO +0.66 to the S&P block. "Just add international" would not meaningfully reduce drawdown risk here.
