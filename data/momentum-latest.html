6. Methodology & per-figure sourcing

Source for every number above: Interactive Brokers TWS market-data API, two calls per ticker — get_price_history (1 year, daily bars, regular trading hours only, retrieved 2026-08-23) and get_price_snapshot (fields: last, prior_close, change, misc_statistics for 13w/26w/52w high-low, avg_90d_usd_volume; queried 2026-08-23, timestamps returned ≈2026-08-21T23:59Z, i.e. Friday's final print — IBKR was not carrying a fresher quote because the market was closed for the weekend at query time).

Derived figures (SMA50, SMA200, RSI14, 1m/3m/6m returns, % vs SMA, % off 52-week high) were computed directly from the raw daily-close series returned by get_price_history using standard formulas (simple moving average; Wilder-style average-gain/average-loss RSI over the trailing 14 sessions; point-to-point % change using the close ~22/~64/~127 trading sessions back for 1m/3m/6m respectively) — not estimated. Each ticker's series was cross-verified against its live snapshot by matching the second-to-last daily close to the snapshot's prior_close field exactly, to guarantee the historical series and the live quote refer to the same instrument before any indicator was computed.

Explicitly unverifiable / unavailable via this data path: IBKR's cumulative_perf_1m, cumulative_perf_ytd, and cumulative_perf_1y snapshot fields returned empty for all 13 individual equities (they populated only for the SPY benchmark, giving YTD +10.09% and 1y +19.44%). Rather than estimate or silently omit momentum context, the 1m/3m/6m returns shown throughout this report were computed independently from the raw price-history series as described above, and are flagged here as self-computed rather than broker-reported for those two fields' would-be equivalents.
